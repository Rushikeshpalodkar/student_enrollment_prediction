# Student Enrollment Prediction System
## Executive Summary for Leadership

### 🎯 Business Overview
This intelligent system predicts student enrollment patterns using advanced machine learning, enabling data-driven decisions for academic planning and resource allocation.

### 💡 Key Business Value
- **Cost Savings**: Optimize resource allocation, preventing over/under-staffing
- **Student Success**: Identify at-risk students early for intervention
- **Strategic Planning**: Forecast enrollment trends 4 semesters ahead
- **Operational Efficiency**: Automate capacity planning and budget allocation
